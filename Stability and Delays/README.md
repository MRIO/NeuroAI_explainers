# Why Delay Kills Stability

## Rationale for NeuroAI students

Delays are unavoidable in biological nervous systems and increasingly important in distributed artificial systems. This explainer shows why even a feedback loop that is stable without delay can become oscillatory or unstable once signals arrive late.

For NeuroAI students, the demo is relevant because it turns a mathematical stability condition into a visual mechanism. It helps connect eigenvalues, feedback, oscillation, and control to neural circuits with axonal conduction delays, synaptic time constants, sensorimotor loops, and recurrent models.
