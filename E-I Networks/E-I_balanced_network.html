Brunel E–I Balance: LIF Network (HTML+JS)

Tune J (excitatory step), g (inhibitory/excitatory ratio), connectivity p, delay D, and external drive Λext. Then click Run to visualize the raster and population rate.

Parameters

Try AI: J≈0.1–0.2 mV, g≈4–6, Λext≈500–2000 sp/s. For synchronous bursts: raise J, lower g a bit, keep a finite delay. For quiescence: raise g or reduce Λext.

What to look for

  • Asynchronous irregular (AI): spikes scattered in raster; rate fairly flat.
  • Synchronous / oscillatory: vertical bands in raster; oscillations in rate trace.
  • Quiescent: activity dies when inhibition dominates or input is too weak.
