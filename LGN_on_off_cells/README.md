# LGN ON/OFF Center-Surround Cells

## Rationale for NeuroAI students

Center-surround receptive fields are a compact example of how neural circuits transform raw sensory input into structured features. This explainer shows why antagonistic ON and OFF channels are not just anatomical details, but computational mechanisms for contrast enhancement, edge sensitivity, and efficient visual coding.

For NeuroAI, the demo is relevant because it connects circuit motifs to representation learning. Students can compare center-surround filtering with convolutional filters, normalization, and feature extraction in artificial vision models, while keeping the biological substrate visible.
