About this simulator

This is a browser port of a small two-neuron recurrent network demo. Each neuron receives recurrent input from both neurons, passes it through either a hyperbolic tangent or logistic sigmoid, and updates either as an Euler step of a continuous system or as a discrete map. The input sign follows the original notebook convention: continuous mode uses W o - theta, while discrete mode uses W o + theta.

The phase view shows the trajectory from the selected initial condition; click inside it to move that initial condition. Optional nullclines mark where one coordinate stops changing; their intersections are fixed points. Hover near a fixed point to see the local eigenvalues and stability classification.

Small recurrent neural networks of this kind were studied extensively by Frank Pasemann and collaborators as minimal models for autonomous neural dynamics and behavior-generating circuits.
