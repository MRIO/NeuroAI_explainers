Leaky integrate & fire — a mechanical analogy

Shishi-odoshi Neuron

A bamboo tube fills from a spout until the water's torque beats the counterweight. It tips, dumps, swings back and clacks — then starts again from empty. The water is a real particle fluid; the trace on the right is the tube's state variable, and the tipping event is the spike.

The tube integrates: C dV/dt = I − gLV, and when V ≥ θ it empties and cannot refill until it has swung back. Turn the flow down far enough and the leak drains the tube as fast as the spout fills it — V settles below threshold and the garden goes quiet, exactly as a leaky neuron stops firing below rheobase.

Built for NB2181 Computational Modeling of Dynamical Systems, TU Delft. Water is a position-based-fluids (SPH) solver; the tube is driven by the real torque of the particles inside it.
